ShopMart bank transfer receipt confirmation update

Flow
- The customer order remains PENDING. The displayed bank information is fictional demo information and cannot receive money.
- An admin reviews the receipt and selects “Confirm receipt received”. This explicit manual action records payment_status=PAID in ShopMart. It does not check a bank or verify funds.
- The admin separately selects “Confirm order”; then existing rider assignment and dispatch steps follow.
- Customer and rider views show “Bank transfer received”; riders are instructed not to collect cash.
- COD behavior is unchanged.

No new database table or column is required. Migration 020_bank_transfer_orders.sql is included to allow BANK_TRANSFER as a payment method. If it has already been applied in Neon, do not rerun it.

Overlay the archive contents onto your ShopMart repository root and review `git diff` before committing.
